# CAMERA

Identify the hero object, active camera and output aspect ratio. Search camera capabilities and inspect their schemas. Set target, focal length and framing without changing unrelated objects. Verify active camera, target visibility, image-space bounds, clipping and intended margins in a preview.

## Failure policy

Stop on an unverified postcondition, preserve evidence, and retry only a scoped correction. Record what was tested and what remains subjective.
