# LIGHTING

Inspect world, lights, camera and render engine. Establish intended key/fill/rim or environment setup and preserve existing rigs unless replacement is requested. Verify light types, positions, energy and color. Render a preview and check highlight clipping, shadow readability and subject separation; histogram checks are diagnostic, not universal aesthetic truth.

## Failure policy

Stop on an unverified postcondition, preserve evidence, and retry only a scoped correction. Record what was tested and what remains subjective.
