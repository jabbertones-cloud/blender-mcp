# ANIMATION

Write a frame-by-frame intent and expected moving objects. Set timeline and keyframes using canonical capabilities. Inspect curves and interpolation. Render or capture start, middle and end frames; verify measurable motion and absence of unexpected jumps. Use motion_qa.py where supported; do not claim it ran without a test result.

## Failure policy

Stop on an unverified postcondition, preserve evidence, and retry only a scoped correction. Record what was tested and what remains subjective.
